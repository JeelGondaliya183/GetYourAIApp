Hi, I'm Jeel

I'm an independent AI consultant focused on large language models, analytics, and forecasting. I help businesses go from "we should use AI" to working prototypes and production systems — without the enterprise bloat.

My work spans LLM-powered assistants (RAG, copilots, automation), decision-ready dashboards, and time-series forecasting for ops and planning teams. I care about maintainable code, honest timelines, and handoffs your team can run after the engagement.

I work remotely with clients worldwide on a project and part-time basis. Engagements typically start with a free discovery call, then a scoped quick win before any larger build.
